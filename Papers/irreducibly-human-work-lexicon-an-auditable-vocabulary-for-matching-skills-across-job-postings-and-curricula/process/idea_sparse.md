# Irreducibly Human Work Lexicon: An Auditable Vocabulary for Describing Work
Research artifact paper, not an AI-automation estimate. Job text and occupational resources describe work at different granularities. A shared lexicon needs to record which labels denote the same concept, how concepts relate, and which individual claims have evidence. Use the named repository, not the similarly titled separate Skills Lexicon snapshot. Present an implemented schema and reproducible descriptive audit, and specify a future human annotation study. If presenting Irreducibly Human Facets, use seven facets rather than tiers and treat job-duty annotation as a proposed separate multiaxis layer, not a field this snapshot already implements, a hierarchy, or a degree of human worth or automation resistance. The seven working labels are Pattern & Association; Embodied & Sensorimotor; Social & Personal; Metacognitive & Supervisory; Causal & Counterfactual; Collective & Distributed; Existential & Wisdom. No empirical generalization about O*NET coverage over these facets is supported by this snapshot. Avoid any claim of objective irreducibility, causal effects, predictive validity, or representativeness.
