## VERDICT

**Major Revision for a contribution-ready resource paper; not yet ready for formal submission.** This paper attempts to show how a vocabulary makes claims reviewable. It delivers an inspectable resource and a verifiable inventory, but not an evaluation of matching accuracy or reviewer benefit. The main audit numbers agree with the pinned records; the most useful next revision is to show where the 1,455 backed claims come from, give one concrete source-match error, and make the proposed validation something another team can execute without guessing. No claim about uniquely human work or automation exposure follows from this audit.

## STRUCTURAL DIAGNOSIS

Reverse outline, by paragraph/gist: **Abstract:** example motivates claim-level backing → pinned counts → validation is future. **§1:** source assertions differ → artifact and scope question. **§2:** descriptors, extraction, linking, provenance. **§3.1:** representation semantics → PostgreSQL worked example. **§3.2:** formal backing and review gates. **§3.3:** reproducible snapshot counting. **§4:** inventories and status, no accuracy. **§5.1:** sample and split → annotation and metrics. **§5.2:** reviewer comparison → separate duty-facet extension. **§6:** limitations → contribution. The main sequence holds; duty facets enter without a definition or example and break the research arc. Move this into a clearly optional future-work paragraph, or define the unit and give one explicitly synthetic instance. The abstract is standalone and scoped; it would be stronger with the provenance split in a short clause, rather than an 8.45% headline whose composition is invisible.

§2 is an adequate orientation to extraction and linking, but it does not yet state what the new resource adds relative to knowledge-organization and mapping standards. Add one paragraph against [SKOS Reference §10](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/#mapping) (already distinguishes exact, close and hierarchical links), [SSSOM](https://mapping-commons.github.io/sssom/dev/) (qualified mappings with provenance), and PROV-O. Frame the contribution as a specific join between a provisionally assembled workforce vocabulary and assertion-level review, not invention of identifiers, relation types or provenance. No reason to claim standards conformance without testing a mapping.

**Future matching study, §5.1.** Separate document/template holdout, in-inventory concepts not seen in development, and truly out-of-inventory cases. A concept absent from training but present in the candidate inventory remains linkable; true OOV is an abstention target. Separate gold-span linking from end-to-end detection and linking, or a change in span detection masquerades as improved identity resolution. Define unit, label space (including no match versus unresolved), comparison budgets/candidate sets, exact match boundary rule, leakage controls and an interval procedure clustered at source family. Negative and ambiguous examples are deliberately oversampled, so aggregate rates describe the benchmark mix unless reweighted to a justified target.

**Future review study, §5.2.** Counterbalance assignment of distinct but comparable claims, not repeated exposure to the same item by the same reviewer. Equalize search tools, time and sources available other than the structured provenance display; document whether display changes what can be retrieved or only its presentation. Define adjudicator access, conflicts and non-resolvable gold cases before testing; report agreement before adjudication. Prespecify the joint quality/time trade-off and sample design (reviewer and item dependence). This is protocol refinement, not data already gathered.

## RESULTS & DISCUSSION

**Restore a failure case (§4/§6).** The pinned [Service orientation](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/irreducibly-human-work-lexicon/blob/3cd82a454877e83d3f7eb989136de1d234e9944e/entries/service-orientation.json) record's O*NET-linked `kind` is the social skill of helping others, while a separately logged Wikipedia description is software architecture. Its match qualifies for backing and is wrong for this sense. The draft's “can provide traceability without guaranteeing correctness” is supported but abstract; this example demonstrates contestability and why the strongest finding stops at provenance. State explicitly that this is one worked failure, not an error-rate sample.

**Primary evidence checks (§2):** [O*NET 30.2 Content Model](https://www.onetcenter.org/dictionary/30.2/excel/content_model_reference.html), [ESCO 1.2.1 Skills pillar](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/classification/skills), [SkillSpan](https://aclanthology.org/2022.naacl-main.366.pdf) abstract and task sections, [job-market entity linking](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-eacl.28.pdf) abstract and framing, [SkiLLens](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-industry.65.pdf) abstract and task framing, and [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/2013/REC-prov-o-20130430/) summary directly support their bounded descriptions. Narrow wording for SkillSpan to “annotates hard- and soft-skill spans in English job postings.” The primary studies do not substantiate lexicon novelty or improvement; the text correctly does not claim it. The Northeastern schedule is a search gateway, not a substitute for individual syllabus evidence; withdrawal of this corpus from the reproduced results is appropriate.

Explain why §6's “should not drive published skill counts” means claims about source corpus/market skill frequencies, not a ban on publishing a count of lexicon entries. Keep the syllabus exercise in limitations or cite the exact predecessor/appendix with availability and license statement. An observation table not released cannot support comparative claims here.

## WRITING AS DESIGN

Make the three levels reusable terms: “observed mention,” “canonical concept” and “claim-level evidence.” Then use them consistently, add O*NET and ESCO expansions if writing for readers outside workforce NLP, and reserve “backed under the rule” for the counted state. “723 developed skills” risks sounding like an assessed capability; “723 records assigned the narrower `skill` kind” is exact. PostgreSQL is a good worked example; give the exact alias and context predicate as data and its separate broader SQL link as an unreviewed policy choice. Avoid implying that one context keyword ensures disambiguation.

## ETHICAL SCREENING

The audited object is public repository data; this review did not see participant records or directly assess any human subjects protocol. Before submission, complete author/contribution, funding, competing-interest and data/reuse declarations, and mark ethics review status accurately rather than assuming exemption. Add a brief license/access note: original project MIT, source-specific O*NET CC BY 4.0 and Wikipedia-adapted CC BY-SA 4.0; keep future syllabus and job excerpts properly cleared and provenance-preserving without exposing student or applicant data. The proposed timing/acceptance reviewer study involves people; seek appropriate institutional determination before recruitment. For the matching study, record source/geography/language boundaries, benchmark family sizes and sampling of omission/slang cases; do not imply observed ads represent all work or that course mentions are learning outcomes.

## WHAT WORKS

§1 and §6 clearly separate audit from effectiveness and refuse to treat opportunity, instruction or occupational averages as proficiency. The exact-expression PostgreSQL example in §3 helps the reader distinguish a literal string from a concept rather than just presenting a schema. §4 correctly separates concepts, status and claim evidence; its observed numbers recalculated exactly. §5 proposes measuring abstention and reporting reviewer disagreement, both important features worth preserving.
